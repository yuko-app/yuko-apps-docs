## Dashboard revenue

The **Home** page shows revenue attributed to your upsells, split by
placement:

- **Post Purchase Revenue**
- **Thank You Revenue**
- **Checkout Revenue**

plus counts of accepted upsells and upsells created, and a **Recent
Upsells** table. Revenue is attributed server-side from Shopify's order
webhooks when an upsell purchase is completed, so figures reflect actual
paid orders (recorded in both your shop currency and the customer's
presentment currency).

## Upsell Events

The **Analytics** page is a full interaction log. Every row is one event:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Upsell Name | Which upsell the event belongs to |
| Type | Post Purchase / Thank You / Checkout |
| Step | Upsell #1 / Upsell #2 / Downsell |
| Event Type | **Shown** / **Accepted** / **Declined** |
| Date | When it happened |

Use it to answer questions like "how often is my downsell shown, and does
anyone accept it?" — compare Shown vs Accepted counts per step to find
weak points in your funnel.

<Note>
  Events appear once customers start interacting with your upsells — a
  freshly published upsell shows an empty log until its first impression.
</Note>
