Checkout offers appear **inside the checkout itself** — for example, under
the order summary — and let customers add the offered product to the order
they're about to place. Accepting adds the item directly to the current
checkout: no second order, no extra payment step.

<Warning>
  Checkout UI blocks require **Shopify Plus**. On other plans this
  placement isn't available — use
  [post-purchase](/post-purchase-upsells/upsells/post-purchase-offers) and
  [thank-you page](/post-purchase-upsells/upsells/thank-you-offers) offers
  instead.
</Warning>

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the checkout block">
    In the checkout editor (**Settings → Checkout → Customize**), on the
    **Checkout** page, add the app's **checkout upsell** block — for
    example below the order summary — and save. See
    [Installation & setup](/post-purchase-upsells/installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the upsell">
    Go to **Upsells → Create upsell** and configure the three wizard
    steps below. The **Checkout Upsell** trigger carries a
    **Shopify Plus** badge in the wizard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use cases & examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Last-chance add-on under the order summary" icon="gift" description="A small, universally useful product at a modest discount.">
    Offer a small, universally useful product (gift wrap, a sample set)
    to every checkout with a modest discount — classic impulse placement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Free-shipping threshold nudge" icon="truck" description="Help carts under $50 reach your free-shipping threshold.">
    Combine a trigger condition **Order subtotal → Lesser than → 50**
    with an attractively priced add-on to help customers reach your
    free-shipping threshold before they place the order.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The block isn't available or doesn't render">
    - Checkout UI blocks require **Shopify Plus** — on other plans the
      block can't be placed in the checkout editor.
    - The block must be manually added on the **Checkout** page in the
      checkout editor and saved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The offer doesn't show during checkout">
    Confirm the upsell's trigger is **Checkout Upsell**, its status is
    **Active**, and its conditions match the cart you're testing with —
    subtotal excludes tax/shipping, total includes them. Only the
    highest-priority matching checkout upsell is shown — see
    [Managing upsells & priority](/post-purchase-upsells/upsells/priority).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The discount didn't apply to the added item">
    The app's discount function applies offer discounts automatically —
    re-save the upsell to re-sync it. Check the whole-number and range
    rules on the discount value.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
