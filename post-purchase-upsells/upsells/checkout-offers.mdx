Checkout offers appear **inside the checkout itself** — for example, under
the order summary — and let customers add the offered product to the order
they're about to place. Because checkout UI extensions require checkout
customization, this placement is available on **Shopify Plus** stores.

## How it works

1. During checkout, the block shows the highest-priority checkout upsell
   whose [trigger](/post-purchase-upsells/upsells/triggers) matches the
   cart.
2. Accepting adds the product — with its discount — directly to the
   current checkout's cart. No second order, no extra payment step.

## Setup

1. In the checkout editor (**Settings → Checkout → Customize**), add the
   app's **checkout upsell** block to the checkout page and save — see
   [Installation & setup](/post-purchase-upsells/installation).
2. Create an upsell with the **Checkout Upsell** trigger (marked with a
   **Shopify Plus** badge in the wizard).

## Shipping discounts

Checkout offers support
[shipping discounts](/post-purchase-upsells/upsells/discounts#shipping-discounts)
on the offered product, applied by the app's automatic discount function.

## Use cases & examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Last-chance add-on under the order summary">
    Offer a small, universally useful product (gift wrap, a sample set) to
    every checkout with a modest discount — classic impulse placement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Free-shipping threshold nudge">
    Combine a trigger condition **Order subtotal → Lesser than → 50** with
    an attractively priced add-on to help customers reach your
    free-shipping threshold before they place the order.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The block isn't available or doesn't render">
    - Checkout UI blocks require **Shopify Plus**. On other plans the
      block can't be placed in the checkout editor.
    - The block must be manually added in the checkout editor — verify
      it's placed on the **Checkout** page and saved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The offer doesn't show during checkout">
    Confirm the upsell's trigger is **Checkout Upsell**, its status is
    **Active**, and its conditions match the cart you're testing with.
    Only the highest-priority matching checkout upsell is shown.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
