Post-purchase offers appear **after the customer pays but before the
thank-you page**. This is the highest-converting placement: the customer
has already committed, and accepting the offer takes **one click** — the
add-on is charged to the payment method they just used, with no re-entry
of payment details.

## How it works

1. The customer completes checkout and pays.
2. Yuko checks your active post-purchase upsells, picks the
   highest-priority one whose [trigger](/post-purchase-upsells/upsells/triggers)
   matches the order, and shows it.
3. Accepting adds the product (with its discount) to the original order.
   Declining continues to the thank-you page — or shows your
   [downsell](/post-purchase-upsells/upsells/funnels) if you've configured
   one.
4. A countdown timer (15 minutes by default) creates urgency; when it
   expires the offer hides itself.

## Setup

Select **Yuko Post Purchase Upsell** in
**Settings → Checkout → Post-purchase page** — see
[Installation & setup](/post-purchase-upsells/installation). Then choose
**Post Purchase Upsell** as the trigger when creating the upsell.

## Use cases & examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Accessory add-on after every order">
    Trigger on **Any products**, offer a low-priced accessory (batteries,
    filters, a care kit) at 10–20% off. Low risk, applies to your whole
    catalog.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Product-specific companion offer">
    Trigger on **Specific products** — the coffee machine — and offer the
    matching beans or descaler. Enable **Exclude products already in
    customer's order** so nobody is offered what they just bought.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Threshold-based premium offer">
    Add a condition **Order total → Greater than → 100** so a premium
    add-on is only offered on high-value orders.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Good to know

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Payment method limitations">
    Shopify only shows post-purchase pages for supported payment flows —
    orders paid via some wallets and third-party gateways skip the
    post-purchase page entirely. If conversion seems low, remember a
    portion of orders never see the offer; this is a platform behavior,
    not a configuration issue.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="One post-purchase app at a time">
    Shopify allows only one app on the post-purchase page. If another
    upsell app is selected in **Settings → Checkout → Post-purchase
    page**, Yuko's offers can't appear until you switch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Only the top matching upsell is shown">
    If several post-purchase upsells are active, the customer sees the
    highest-priority one that matches their order — not all of them. Use
    **Manage priority** on the Upsells page to control the order. See
    [Managing upsells & priority](/post-purchase-upsells/upsells/priority).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
