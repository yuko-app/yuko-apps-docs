Post-purchase offers appear **after the customer pays but before the
thank-you page**. This is the highest-converting placement: the customer
has already committed, and accepting takes **one click** — the add-on is
charged to the payment method they just used, with no re-entry of payment
details. Declining (or letting the countdown expire) simply continues to
the thank-you page.

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the post-purchase page">
    Shopify allows exactly one app on the post-purchase page. Select
    **Yuko Post Purchase Upsell** in
    **Settings → Checkout → Post-purchase page** — the dashboard Setup
    Guide's **Enable** button takes you there, and **Refresh status**
    confirms it. See [Installation & setup](/post-purchase-upsells/installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the upsell">
    Go to **Upsells → Create upsell** and configure the three wizard
    steps below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use cases & examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Accessory add-on after every order">
    Trigger on **Any products**, offer a low-priced accessory (batteries,
    filters, a care kit) at 10–20% off. Low risk, applies to your whole
    catalog.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Product-specific companion offer">
    Trigger on **Specific products** — the coffee machine — and offer the
    matching beans or descaler. Enable **Exclude products already in
    customer's order** so nobody is offered what they just bought.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Threshold-based premium offer">
    Add a condition **Order total → Greater than → 100** so a premium
    add-on is only offered on high-value orders.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Downsell to a cheaper alternative">
    If the $49 add-on is declined, the downsell offers a $19 version — or
    the same product at 25% off instead of 10%.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The offer doesn't appear after payment">
    Work through these in order:

    1. **Is the app selected as the post-purchase app?** Check
       **Settings → Checkout → Post-purchase page** — only one app can be
       selected there.
    2. **Is the upsell published and Active?** Drafts and paused upsells
       never show.
    3. **Do the trigger conditions match your test order?** With the
       **AND** connector, every condition must pass — and remember
       subtotal excludes tax/shipping while total includes them.
    4. **Payment method** — Shopify skips the post-purchase page for some
       payment flows (certain wallets and third-party gateways). Test
       with a standard card payment; this is platform behavior, not a
       configuration issue.
    5. **Priority** — only the highest-priority matching upsell shows.
       See [Managing upsells & priority](/post-purchase-upsells/upsells/priority).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The discount didn't apply">
    The app maintains its discount function automatically. Re-save the
    upsell to force a re-sync and test again; if it persists, contact
    support. Also check the value rules — whole numbers only, percentage
    1–100, amounts not exceeding the product price.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Upsell #2 or the downsell never appears">
    Check their toggles in the Offer step and that each enabled step has
    products selected. The downsell needs an explicit decline — an
    expired timer ends the funnel silently.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The offer disappeared while the customer was reading it">
    The countdown **Timer** hides the offer when it expires (default 15
    minutes). Adjust it in the Style step's Message section — see
    [Styling your offer](/post-purchase-upsells/customization/styling).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
