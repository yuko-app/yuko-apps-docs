Every upsell can grow into a small funnel in the **Offer** step. Beyond the
primary offer ("Upsell 1"), you can add:

- **Upsell offer #2** — shown *if the customer accepts* the first offer
- **Downsell offer** — shown *if the customer declines* the first offer

```text
        Offer shown
        /          \
   accepted      declined
      |               |
  Upsell #2       Downsell
```

Each step has its own products, its own discounts, and its own styling —
the Style step shows **Primary**, **Secondary**, and **Downsell** sections
with preview tabs for **Upsell**, **Upsell #2**, and **Downsell (if
declined)**.

## Setting up a funnel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure Upsell 1">
    Select products and discounts for the primary offer as usual.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Upsell offer #2 (optional)">
    Toggle on **Upsell offer #2** ("Create the offer you will be showing
    after customer accepts first upsell") and select its products and
    discounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Downsell offer (optional)">
    Toggle on **Downsell offer** ("Create the offer you will be showing
    after customer declines first upsell") and configure it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Style each step">
    In the Style step, customize each section and check all three preview
    tabs before **Save and publish**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use cases & examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Classic ladder: accessory → refill pack">
    Upsell 1 offers an accessory at 15% off. If accepted, Upsell #2
    offers a refill bundle — the customer has proven they're in a buying
    mood.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Downsell to a cheaper alternative">
    If the $49 add-on is declined, the downsell offers a $19 version or a
    deeper discount on the same product. A decline becomes a second
    chance instead of a dead end.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Downsell with a stronger discount">
    Offer the same product in the downsell but move from 10% to 25% off —
    a classic recovery pattern. Keep the stronger discount exclusive to
    the downsell so it retains its urgency.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Upsell #2 or the downsell never appears">
    - Both are **off by default** — check their toggles in the Offer
      step, and that each enabled step has at least one product selected
      (the wizard validates this on save).
    - The downsell only shows after an explicit **decline** of the first
      offer. If the timer expires or the customer navigates away, the
      funnel ends without a downsell.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Styling looks right for Upsell 1 but wrong for the others">
    Each funnel step is styled independently in the Style step — select
    the **Secondary** or **Downsell** section and check its own preview
    tab.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
