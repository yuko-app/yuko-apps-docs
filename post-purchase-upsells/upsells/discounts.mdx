Each product in an offer carries its own discount, set in the **Offer**
step. Discounts are applied automatically — the app maintains its own
Shopify discount function, so there are no codes for customers to enter.

## Product discount types

| Type | What it does | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No discount | Product is offered at its normal price | — |
| Discounted in percentage | Percentage off the price | 15 → 15% off |
| In amount | Fixed amount off the price | 5 → $5 off |
| Specific | Sell at an exact price | 10 → costs exactly $10 |

Rules to know:

- Discount values must be **whole numbers** — no decimals.
- Amount and specific-price discounts can't exceed the product's original
  price.
- **Use compare at price** shows the product's compare-at price as the
  strikethrough reference instead of its regular price.
- **Use custom discount title** sets the discount name that appears on
  the order (useful for tidy order records and customer-facing receipts).

## Shipping discounts

Thank-you page and checkout offers can also discount shipping on the
offered product:

| Type | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Default shipping | Normal shipping rates — no discount |
| Free shipping | Shipping is free (100% off) |
| Percentage discount | A percentage off shipping, e.g. 50% |
| Amount discount | A fixed amount off shipping, e.g. $5 off |

Each option supports an optional **Shipping message** shown on the offer —
e.g. "Free Shipping!" or "50% off shipping". Leave it blank to use the
default wording.

<Note>
  Post-purchase offers are added to the original order, so shipping
  discounts apply to the **thank-you page and checkout** placements —
  where a shipping cost is actually being calculated for the offer.
</Note>

## Use cases & examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title='A clean "$10 add-on" offer'>
    Use **Specific** pricing so the offer reads as a flat price rather
    than a math exercise — "Add this for just $10" converts better than
    "23% off $12.99".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bigger-looking savings with compare-at price">
    If your product has a compare-at price set in Shopify, enable **Use
    compare at price** — the strikethrough shows the compare-at value, so
    the displayed saving includes the existing markdown.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Free shipping to remove second-order friction">
    On thank-you offers, pair a modest product discount with **Free
    shipping** — paying shipping twice is the top reason customers
    decline a post-order offer.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The discount isn't applied at purchase">
    The app creates and syncs its discount function automatically when
    it's installed and offers are saved. If a discount isn't applying,
    re-save the upsell to trigger a re-sync, then test again. If it
    persists, contact support — we can re-sync the discount function for
    your store.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The form rejects my discount value">
    Values must be whole numbers (no decimals), percentages must be
    between 1 and 100, and amount/specific values can't exceed the
    product's original price — the validation message names the limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The saving badge shows the wrong amount">
    If **Use compare at price** is on, savings are calculated against the
    compare-at price, not the current price. Check the product's
    compare-at value in Shopify.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
