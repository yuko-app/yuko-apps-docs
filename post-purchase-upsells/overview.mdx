Yuko Post Purchase Upsells shows discounted **one-click add-on offers** to
shoppers at three points in the buying flow:

- **Post-purchase page** — after payment, before the thank-you page, with
  true one-click acceptance (no re-entering payment details)
- **Thank-you / order status page** — a product offer shown after checkout
- **Checkout page** — an in-checkout offer (Shopify Plus)

## What you can do

- Build upsells in a simple **Trigger → Offer → Style** wizard
- Target offers by products, collections, order value, country, and more
- Add a second upsell or a **downsell** shown when the first offer is declined
- Apply product discounts and **shipping discounts** to offers
- Add urgency with a countdown timer
- Track shown, accepted, and declined offers plus revenue by placement
