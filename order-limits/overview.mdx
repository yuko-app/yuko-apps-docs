Yuko Order Limits lets you enforce **minimum and maximum purchase rules**
on your storefront: minimum order quantities (MOQ), maximum quantity caps,
order value floors and ceilings, weight limits, and "sell in multiples"
pack rules.

Limit messages appear on your product pages, cart drawer, and cart page,
and limits are enforced at checkout automatically.

## What you can do

- **Product limits** — min/max quantity per product or variant, sell in multiples
- **Collection limits** — quantity or value limits across one or more collections
- **Order limits** — cart-wide quantity, value, or weight rules
- **Targeting** — apply rules by customer tag or country, with exclusions
- **Scheduling** — run limits between start and end dates
- **Custom messages** — translate limit messages into 13+ languages
