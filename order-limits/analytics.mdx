The dashboard's **Overview** section shows two metrics:

## Impressions

The number of times Yuko Order Limits has successfully **blocked an invalid
action** — a customer trying to exceed a maximum, check out below a
minimum, or break a multiples rule. Rising impressions mean your limits are
actively doing their job.

## Contacts

The total number of **contacts collected via Order Limits** — customers
who shared their details after hitting a limit.

## Active limits at a glance

Below the metrics, the dashboard lists your currently active limits and the
live status of the theme extension pieces (the **Active App Block(s)**
card), so you can spot configuration problems without leaving the page.
