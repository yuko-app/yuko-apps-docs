The app-level **Settings** page has five tabs: **General**, **Referral
& Affiliate Dashboard**, **Branding**, **Email Domains**, and
**Integrations**. (Each program also has its own Settings tab under
Referral and Affiliate — those are covered in their sections.)

## General

- **Referral Program** / **Affiliate Program** — master toggles for
  each program. At least one must stay enabled. *(Affiliate requires
  Starter.)*
- **Translations** — entry points to translate the referral pieces,
  affiliate pieces, and the account portal per storefront locale.
- **Calculate Reward or Commissions** — whether rewards and commissions
  are calculated on the **Order total (including taxes & shipping)** or
  the **Subtotal (excluding taxes & shipping)**.
- **Enable Referral And Affiliate Sale Tag** — tags attributed orders
  as "Referral and Affiliate Sale - Shopjar", so they're filterable in
  Shopify orders and reports. *(Essential)*
- **Enable IP based Advanced Fraud Protection** — IP tracking to detect
  self-referrals. *(Growth)*

<Tip>
  Calculate commissions on the **Subtotal**. Paying commission on
  shipping and taxes quietly inflates every payout — most programs
  never intend that.
</Tip>

## Referral & Affiliate Dashboard

The [member portal](/shopjar/member-portal): your portal URL, the
portal customizer, **Allow Email OTP for Guest Login**, and the OTP
page/email customizers.

## Branding

**Store Branding** sets the logo and colors used across popups, pages,
forms, and emails. Choose a template — Default, Coral Pink, Herbal
Green, Golden Ochre, Crimson Red, Expresso Brown, Pacific Blue, Modern
Gray — or set custom **Primary Color**, **Primary text color**, and
**Secondary text color**, plus your logo (JPG/PNG). On save, choose
which surfaces to update: Reward Popup, Referral Widget, Landing page,
Affiliate signup form, and Email notifications. *(Essential)*
