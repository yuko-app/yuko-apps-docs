Both integrations live under **Settings → Integrations** and require
the **Growth** plan.

## Klaviyo

Syncs your referral and affiliate members and their events into Klaviyo
to power automated, personalized email flows.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your Klaviyo key">
    In Klaviyo's account settings, create a **Private API Key** with
    the access level set to **"Grant applications full access"** for
    every API scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure">
    Click **Configure** on the Klaviyo card, paste the key, turn on
    **Enable Klaviyo Integration**, and save. The card badge flips to
    **Active** once verified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  A high-impact first flow: trigger on new referral members and send
  their referral link a week after joining — reactivating members who
  joined but never shared.
</Tip>

## Shopify Flow

Connects Shopjar's triggers and actions to Shopify Flow, so you can
automate on program events in your Shopify admin — for example, tagging
customers when they become affiliates, or posting to Slack on big
referral sales.

Use **Send test event** to fire a sample of every Flow event with
placeholder data — safe to run — and verify your workflows end to end.
