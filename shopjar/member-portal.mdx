The portal is a "My Account" page for your program members — referrers
and affiliates alike. It answers the question you'd otherwise get by
email every day: *"Where do I see my rewards?"*

Your portal URL is shown under **Settings → Referral & Affiliate
Dashboard** (it ends in `/my-account`) — link it from your store's
footer or navigation. *(Essential plan)*

## What members see

- **Login** — password-free, via email OTP: the member enters their
  email, receives a one-time code, and is in. Enable this under
  **Allow Email OTP for Guest Login**. Only approved members receive
  codes.
- **Referral tab** — their referral link with share buttons, Total
  Sales and Total Rewards stats, and a reward history table.
- **Affiliate tab** — their affiliate link and coupons, stats
  (Commission Balance, Total Earnings, Total Paid), a transactions
  table, plus self-service actions:
  - **Convert as coupon** / **Convert as storeCredit** — claim part or
    all of their balance instantly (when you've enabled these payout
    options)
  - **PayPal email capture** — affiliates enter and update the email
    you'll pay them at

## Customizing the portal

**Settings → Referral & Affiliate Dashboard → Customize Referrer /
Affiliate dashboard page** opens a full editor with Edit/Preview and
Desktop/Mobile views. Every text on the page is editable — headings,
tab names, stat labels, table headers, empty-state messages, the claim
popups, and Custom CSS. The OTP login page and OTP email have their own
customizers (**Customize OTP Page**, **Customize OTP Email**).

For multi-language stores, **Manage Account Translations** (Settings →
General → Translations) translates the portal per locale.

## Headless & custom pages

Running headless, or want the portal inside an existing page?
**Affiliate → Settings → Embedded Pages** provides copyable embed URLs
for the affiliate portal and signup form.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A member can't log in">
    The OTP email only goes to **approved** members — pending or
    rejected applicants see "you are not part of our referral /
    affiliate program". Check their status, and that OTP guest login
    is enabled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A member sees a blocked message">
    "Your Account has been Blocked" means exactly that — unblock them
    from the member profile if it was a mistake. "Your
    referral/affiliate program is disabled" appears when the program
    toggle is off in Settings → General.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
