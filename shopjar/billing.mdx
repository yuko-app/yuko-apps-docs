Shopjar has four plans, managed on the **Billing** page. Every paid
plan starts with a **14-day free trial** — you're not charged during
the trial, and you can cancel anytime.

| Plan | Price | In short |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Customer referrals with unique links, popup & floating widgets, unlimited members/orders/rewards, email notifications |
| Essential | $7/mo | Full referral program: safe referral links, signup forms & landing pages, thank-you widget, automated rewards, coupon prefix, branding |
| Starter | $12/mo | Everything in Essential + the **affiliate program**: influencer management, commission tracking, offline/coupon/store-credit payouts, signup forms, self-managed affiliate portal, basic fraud protection |
| Growth | $29/mo | Everything in Starter + PayPal & Wise cash payouts, scheduled automatic PayPal payouts, Tremendous (700+ reward types), advanced fraud protection (IP + referral OTP), Klaviyo, Shopify Flow, branded emails & custom sending domain, white-glove onboarding |

Features that need a higher plan show a lock icon with an upgrade link
right where they live (e.g. "Included in Starter") — so you always know
what an upgrade unlocks.

Commonly asked gates:

- **Affiliate program** — Starter
- **Branded referral links & link destination** — Starter
- **PayPal / Wise / Tremendous payouts, scheduled payouts** — Growth
- **Klaviyo, Shopify Flow, custom sending domain** — Growth
- **IP fraud protection & referral OTP** — Growth

## Discount codes

Have a partner discount code? Enter it under **Apply Discount Code** on
the Billing page — applied codes show as a percentage off all charges.
You can also book a call for a special discount via the banner.

## Downgrading & cancelling

- **Revert to free** — if you have active rewards that need a paid plan
  (referrer rewards, rules with conditions, free-shipping or
  free-product rewards), the app lists exactly which rules to update or
  remove before reverting.
- **Cancelling** — uninstalling the app ends billing.

<Warning>
  If you cancel with active members, their referral links and coupon
  codes stop working. Pause your promotion before cancelling so
  members aren't left sharing dead links.
</Warning>
