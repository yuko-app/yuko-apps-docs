Shopjar turns your customers into referrers and your creators into
affiliates — in one app. Configure reward rules, publish storefront
widgets and signup pages, and Shopjar automatically tracks attributed
orders, issues rewards, and pays out commissions.

## What you can do

**Referral program**

- Reward customers who refer friends, and the friends they refer
- Unique referral links, referral codes, and branded links
- Fraud protection with email verification and IP checks

**Affiliate program**

- Affiliate signup forms with approve/reject workflows
- Flexible commission rules and tiers
- A self-service **member portal** where affiliates see their links,
  sales, rewards, and commissions

**Payouts**

- Pay commissions via PayPal, Wise, Tremendous, store credit, or coupons
- Individual, bulk, and scheduled automatic payouts

**And more**

- Customizable emails with your own sending domain
- Analytics and reports with CSV export
- Klaviyo and Shopify Flow integrations
