<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Install
    [Shopjar Referral & Affiliate Marketing](https://apps.shopify.com/referral-and-affiliates)
    from the Shopify App Store. Every paid plan starts with a **14-day
    free trial** — you're not charged during the trial, and you can
    cancel anytime by uninstalling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the onboarding wizard">
    On first open, Shopjar walks you through:

    1. **Choose a plan** — Essential ($7), Starter ($12, most popular),
       or Growth ($29). See [Billing & plans](/shopjar/billing).
    2. **Business Details** — your name, email, and phone, plus an
       anti-spam confirmation.
    3. **Referral Program Setup Wizard** — pick the referrer reward and
       the friend reward (you can change these anytime later).
    4. **Affiliate Program Set Up Wizard** — pick the commission type:
       paid as a commission (cash) or paid as a coupon.
    5. **Overview** — review your choices and **Start your 14-day free
       trial**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the dashboard Setup Guide">
    The dashboard's **Setup Guide** takes your program live:

    1. **Activate app** — turn on the Shopjar app embed in your theme
       editor so referral widgets appear on your storefront. Use
       **Refresh status** to confirm.
    2. **Enable Referral Landing Page** — creates a dedicated page
       where customers discover your program.
    3. **Enable Safe Referral Reward Popup** — captures and verifies a
       friend's email before unlocking their reward, stopping abuse
       before it starts.

    <Check>
      When the guide completes you'll see "🎉 Your Referral Program is
      Ready!" with your landing page link — share it in emails, SMS,
      and social media.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Prefer a hands-off setup? Our team offers free white-glove setup —
  click **Book a free setup call** on the dashboard and we'll configure
  your program with you.
</Tip>
