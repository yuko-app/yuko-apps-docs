Shopjar sends automated emails on both sides of the program. Each has
an on/off toggle and a full template editor — find them under
**Referral → Emails** and **Affiliate → Emails**.

## Referral emails (5)

| Email | Sent when | Default subject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invitation email | A referrer invites a friend | You've got a gift |
| Friend reward claim | A friend claims their reward | You've claimed your referral reward |
| Welcome email | Someone joins the referral program | Get 10% off your next purchase and help a friend save too! |
| Referrer reward | A referrer earns a reward | You have earned a reward for successful referral |
| Admin: referral sale | A referral drives an order | You've made a successful sale! |

The invitation email can **include the coupon code** directly, so the
friend's reward is one tap away.

## Affiliate emails (7)

| Email | Sent when |
| --- | --- |
| Admin: affiliate sale | An affiliate sale is made |
| Admin: application received | Someone applies to the program |
| Application approved | You approve an affiliate |
| Application rejected | You reject an application |
| Commission earned | An affiliate earns a commission |
| Payout processed | A payout completes |
| Commission redeemed as coupon | An affiliate converts balance to a coupon |

## Customizing a template

Each editor has **Edit / Preview** tabs and a Live/Draft status, with
sections for the **Image**, **Subject**, **Title**, **Message**,
**Button** (text, URL, colors), and **Footer**.

Templates support shortcodes — the editor lists what's available per
email. Highlights: `{{referrer_name}}`, `{{friend_name}}`,
`{{affiliate_name}}`, `{{referral_url}}`, `{{referral_code}}`,
`{{coupon_code}}`, `{{coupon_expiry_date}}`, `{{paid_amount}}`,
`{{order_value}}`, `{{affiliate_dashboard_url}}`, `{{store_name}}`,
and `{{store_url}}`.

<Tip>
  Point the reward email's **Button Url** at
  `https://yourstore.com/discount/{{coupon_code}}` — one click applies
  the coupon and lands the member in your store, no copy-paste.
</Tip>

<Note>
  New stores have email sending limited to **10 per day** until
  verified — a short anti-spam measure. Book a quick verification call
  (via the help footer below) to unlock full sending.
</Note>

## Use cases & examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Quiet admin inbox" icon="bell-slash" description="Keep member emails on, turn admin notifications off at volume.">
    The two admin emails are great early on and noisy at scale — toggle
    them off once you're checking the dashboard daily instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="On-brand from day one" icon="palette" description="Store branding applies to email templates too.">
    Set your logo and colors in **Settings → Branding** and apply them
    to **Email notifications** in the confirmation modal — every
    template picks up your look.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
