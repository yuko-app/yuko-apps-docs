**Affiliate → Sales & Commissions** carries the affiliate program's
numbers, with time filters from Today to All Time.

## Stat cards & analytics panel

**Total Revenue**, **Total Earnings**, **Total Paid Commissions**, and
**Total scheduled payouts**, plus an affiliate revenue chart and an
Analytics panel breaking down Sales, Earnings, Paid Commissions,
Reverted Commissions, Payouts, Schedule Payouts, and Pending Requests.

## Reports

- **Sales** — every affiliate-attributed order: date, order ID,
  customer, affiliate, amount, and status (**Paid**, **Fraud**, **Fraud
  via IP**), with **Export** to CSV.
- **Commission Earnings** — the full
  [commission ledger](/shopjar/affiliate/commissions#the-commission-ledger)
  with statuses and bulk actions.
- **Per-member stats** — each member profile shows their Sales,
  Revenue, Earnings, Paid, and Unpaid Commission, with payout and
  transaction history.

<Tip>
  Review **Total Earnings vs Total Paid Commissions** monthly — the gap
  is your outstanding liability. If it keeps growing, lower your
  scheduled-payout threshold or nudge affiliates to claim as store
  credit.
</Tip>
