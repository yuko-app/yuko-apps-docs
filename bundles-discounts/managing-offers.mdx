## The Offers page

The **Offers** page lists every offer with status tabs (**All / Active /
Inactive**), a **Search offers** field, and a date-range filter (**All
time / Today / Last 7 days / Last 30 days / Last 90 days**) that scopes
the analytics columns.

Summary tiles at the top show **Impressions**, **Conversions**, and
**Revenue** for the selected range; the table adds per-offer columns —
Title, Type, Impressions, Conversions, Conversion rate, Offer revenue,
Status, Actions (Edit / Delete).

## Status

The status pill toggles each offer between **Active** and **Inactive**
directly from the list. On the offer page, **Duplicate**, **Deactivate**,
and **Delete** live under the secondary actions — duplication is only
available there, not in the list.

<Warning>
  A red **Not Visible** badge next to the status means the offer can't
  render: the **Yuko Extension** app embed (or the offer's theme block)
  is disabled in your theme. Fix it via
  [Installation & theme setup](/bundles-discounts/installation).
</Warning>

Deleting shows a confirmation (*"Are you sure you want to delete this
offer? This action cannot be undone."*) — for Bundle Builder offers this
also deletes the auto-created page.

## Offer overlap

Two active offers covering the same products conflict. The editor shows a
critical banner — **"Oops! Yuko identified issue..."** — naming the
conflict (e.g. *"You already have active deal covering all products"*).
Resolve it by narrowing one offer's **When to display...** rule or
deactivating one of the offers; the overlapping offer won't behave
predictably until you do.

## Editing

The offer name is edited inline on the offer page (pencil icon next to
the title — the badge beside it shows the offer type). Validation errors
list in a banner (*"There are N errors with this offer"*), switch you to
the tab that needs attention, and tag tier-scoped problems with the tier
number.
