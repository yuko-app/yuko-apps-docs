<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My offer doesn't show on the storefront">
    Work through these in order:

    1. **Yuko Extension enabled?** A red **Not Visible** badge in the
       Offers list means the app embed (or required block) is off in
       your theme — the app also warns after creating an offer: *"To
       ensure the offers are displayed properly on product page,
       activate Yuko Extension in your theme settings."*
    2. **Offer Active?** Check the status pill; scheduled campaigns are
       inactive outside their dates.
    3. **Targeting** — does the product actually match the **When to
       display...** rule? Smart collections can surprise you.
    4. **Overlap** — if another active offer covers the same products,
       the editor's "Oops! Yuko identified issue..." banner names the
       conflict.
    5. **Eligibility rules** — tag/spend/order-count rules only match
       logged-in customers; a logged-out visitor may be excluded by
       design.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The discount doesn't apply at checkout">
    - **Combinations**: if the customer's cart carries another discount
      of a class your deal doesn't combine with, one of the two won't
      apply.
    - **Fixed amounts** larger than the product price block the add to
      cart entirely.
    - For tiered offers, confirm the cart actually satisfies a tier
      range — and watch for gaps between ranges.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Offer shows on the wrong products">
    Check the **Applies to:** mode carefully — the two "except" modes
    (products / collections) invert the selection, and tag mode matches
    any listed tag. For bundles, remember **When to display...** (where
    the widget shows) is independent of the bundle contents or the
    eligible pool.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The widget position looks wrong in my theme">
    Widgets auto-mount at the add-to-cart form (the embed's **Mount
    selector**, default `form[action*="/cart/add"]`). For exact control,
    add the matching app block (Yuko Discount / Yuko Bundle / Yuko
    Related Products / Yuko Upsell) to your product template — see the
    [storefront blocks reference](/bundles-discounts/storefront-blocks).
    If your theme's form doesn't match the default selector, contact
    support before editing it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Bundle Builder page is empty or missing">
    The page is created on first save and published only while the offer
    is **Active**. If you deleted the page in Shopify, re-save the offer
    — the app recreates it. See
    [Build Your Own Box](/bundles-discounts/offers/bundle-builder).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the 'Test Offer' in my Offers list?">
    A draft AI Bundle seeded at install so you can explore the offer
    editor. It's inactive by default — activate it, repurpose it, or
    delete it freely.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Analytics show zero impressions">
    Impressions count only when the widget renders — the usual cause is
    the Yuko Extension embed being disabled or the offer not matching
    any visited product page. See
    [Analytics](/bundles-discounts/analytics).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can offers show only for certain customers?">
    Yes — quantity breaks, quantity discounts, and volume discounts have
    **Customer eligibility rules** (status, tags, country, amount spent,
    orders completed). Tag/spend/order rules require the customer to be
    logged in.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
