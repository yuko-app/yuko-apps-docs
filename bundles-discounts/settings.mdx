The **Settings** page manages where the app sends important updates.

- **Store email** — read-only, pulled from your Shopify store settings
  (the page links to instructions for changing it in Shopify).
- **Additional contacts** — click **Add email address** to add more
  recipients; every address receives important updates, and each row has
  a **Remove** button.

Click **Save** to apply.

<Note>
  All offer-level configuration (design, scheduling, eligibility,
  combinations) lives inside each offer's editor — see the offer type
  guides. There is no global styling setting.
</Note>
