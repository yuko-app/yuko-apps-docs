This guide creates the most popular offer type — **Quantity Breaks & Free
Gifts** — from a ready-made template.

<Info>
  Make sure the **Yuko Extension** app embed is enabled first — see
  [Installation & theme setup](/bundles-discounts/installation).
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the offer">
    Click **Create Offer**. On the **Select offer** screen, choose
    **Quantity Breaks & Free Gifts** ("Buy More, Save More offers. Price
    updates automatically as customers choose quantity").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a template">
    The **Select template** gallery shows live previews — pick a **Color
    theme** with the swatch row (or **Custom**), then click **Select** on
    a template. You can fully customize it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the products">
    In the **When to display...** card, keep **All products** or narrow
    it — for a first test, pick **Specific selected products** and choose
    one product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the tiers">
    The template seeds tiers like *Buy 1 / Buy 2 / Buy 3*. For each tier
    set the **Quantity**, **Discount type** (Percentage / Fixed amount /
    Final tier price), and **Discount value** — e.g. 2 → 10% off,
    3 → 15% off. Mark one tier **Selected by default**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The offer is created and the **Product page preview**
    shows exactly what customers will see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your storefront">
    Open a targeted product page.

    <Check>
      The tier table renders near the add-to-cart button; selecting a
      tier updates the price, and the discount applies automatically in
      cart and checkout.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>
