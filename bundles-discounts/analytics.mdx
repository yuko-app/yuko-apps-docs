Analytics appear in two places: summary tiles and per-offer columns on
the **Offers** page (scoped by its date-range filter), and offer counts
on the dashboard.

| Metric | How it's counted |
| --- | --- |
| Impressions | Once per offer per page view, when the widget actually renders on the storefront |
| Conversions | Recorded when a paid order is attributed to the offer (from Shopify's order webhooks) |
| Conversion rate | Conversions ÷ impressions, per offer |
| Revenue | The attributed order value from paid orders, recorded in the order currency and your store currency |

Notes for interpreting the numbers:

- Impressions require the widget to render — if the **Yuko Extension**
  embed is off, you get zero impressions, not zero interest.
- Conversions and revenue come from **paid** orders only; abandoned
  checkouts never count.
- The summary tiles include offers you've since deleted; the per-offer
  table shows live offers.
