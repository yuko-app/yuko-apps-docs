Yuko Bundle Builder & Discounts lets you create **promotional offers** that
display directly on your product pages — no discount codes needed. Discounts
apply automatically at checkout.

## Offer types

- **Quantity breaks & free gifts** — "buy more, save more" tier tables,
  with optional free gifts per tier
- **Quantity discounts** — per-product quantity tiers
- **Volume discount bundles** — cart-wide tiers across a collection
- **Fixed bundles** — buy specific products together for a discount
- **Mix & match** — let customers build a bundle from a product pool
- **Build your own box** — a dedicated bundle-builder page on your store
- **Related products** — discounted cross-sell offers

## What you can do

- Style offers with ready-made templates and color themes, with live preview
- Target offers to all products, specific products, collections, or tags
- Schedule campaigns and set customer eligibility rules
- Track impressions, conversions, and revenue per offer
