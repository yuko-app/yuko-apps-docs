Related Products renders a **"You may also like" block** on product pages
— each suggested product with its own add-to-cart button and an optional
discount. Suggestions can come from Shopify's recommendations (no
maintenance) or from products/collections you pick.

**Create it:** **Create Offer → Bundle → Related Products**. Tabs:
**Offers block**, **Design**, and **Settings** (this type has no
Deal-settings card — just combinations and schedule).

### What to display

Where the suggestions come from:

- **AI Recommendition (Recommended)** — Shopify's Product
  Recommendations API picks related products live; nothing to maintain
- **All products except selected**
- **All products except selected collections**
- **Products in selected collections**
- **Specific selected products**

Switching the source clears the previous selection.

### Discount

**None**, **Percentage discount**, or **Fixed amount for each product**,
with the **Value** field.

## Design tab

This type's widget text lives on the Design tab: **Block title** (default
`You may also like`) and **CTA button text** (default `Add`). Then:
**Themes** carousel, **Borders** (block / product image / CTA border
sizes and radii), **Spacing** (inside/outside top and bottom, 0–60),
**Colors** (including **Next product button** for the carousel arrow),
and **Typography**.

## Use cases & examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Zero-maintenance cross-sell" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" description="AI recommendations storewide with 5% off to nudge the click.">
    **Applies to: All products** + **AI Recommendition** + **Percentage
    discount** `5`. One offer, every page, always relevant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Curated accessories under gear pages" icon="screwdriver-wrench" description="Show the Accessories collection on your equipment pages.">
    Target the equipment collection in **When to display...**, source
    **Products in selected collections** → Accessories.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tag-driven placement" icon="tags" description="Show the block only on products tagged “clearance”.">
    Use **Products with specific tags** with the `clearance` tag —
    placement follows your tagging workflow automatically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The block doesn't appear">
    Check the **Yuko Extension** embed (red **Not Visible** badge =
    disabled), the **When to display...** rule, and offer status. For
    manual placement, the theme editor block is **Yuko Related
    Products** on product templates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AI mode shows nothing on some pages">
    Shopify needs recommendation data for the viewed product — young
    stores or new products may not have it yet. Switch the source to a
    curated mode for those cases.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The discount doesn't apply to added items">
    Check **Combinations** against the rest of the cart, and remember
    **None** is a valid source setting — the block can run without any
    discount.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
