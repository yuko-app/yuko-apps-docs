The AI Bundle builds itself: on the storefront it fills the bundle's
slots with **Shopify's product recommendations for the product being
viewed**, so every product page gets a relevant, automatically chosen
bundle. You define the slot count, the discount, and optional **fallback
products** used when recommendations aren't available (or are sold out).

<Tip>
  A draft AI Bundle named **Test Offer** is seeded automatically when you
  install the app — open it from the Offers list to explore the type
  without starting from scratch.
</Tip>

**Create it:** **Create Offer → Bundle → AI Bundle** (the highlighted
card with the magic icon). Tabs: **Offers block**, **Design**,
**Settings & Schedule**.

## Offers block tab

### When to display

Which product pages show the AI bundle:

<WhenToDisplay />

### Offer title

Same title field with eye toggle and horizontal-line checkbox.

### Number of products

The **Slots** field (allowed **1–4**, default 3) — how many recommended
products the bundle includes.

### Fallback products

Expand **Fallback products** to *"pick products the AI can fall back to
when it can't find a best bundle"*. Fallbacks are optional, but if you
select any, the count must equal the slot count — the form enforces
*"Please select {n} fallback product(s) (or leave it empty)."*

### Discount

**None**, **Percentage discount**, or **Fixed amount for each product**,
plus the **Value** field.

## Use cases & examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Storewide personalized bundles" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" description="Every product page gets its own auto-built 3-product bundle.">
    **Applies to: All products**, 3 slots, 10% off. One offer covers the
    whole catalog with page-relevant bundles — the lowest-effort AOV
    play in the app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Safety net for a young store" icon="life-ring" description="Fallback products cover pages with no recommendation data yet.">
    New stores may lack recommendation history. Select 3 fallback
    products (matching the slot count) — bestsellers work well — so the
    widget is never empty.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The bundle shows different products than expected">
    That's the point — products come live from Shopify's recommendations
    for the viewed product, preferring in-stock items and skipping the
    current product. To pin exact products, use a
    [Fixed Bundle](/bundles-discounts/offers/fixed-bundle) instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The widget is empty on some pages">
    Those pages likely have no recommendations yet and no fallbacks are
    set — add fallback products (count must equal slots).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It doesn't render at all">
    Check the **Yuko Extension** embed, the **When to display...**
    targeting, offer status (the seeded **Test Offer** starts as a
    draft), and the overlap banner.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
