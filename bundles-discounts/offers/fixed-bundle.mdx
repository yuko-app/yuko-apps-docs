A Fixed Bundle offers a **set list of products, bought together for a
discount** — "camera + bag + SD card, 10% off". The bundle widget renders
on the product pages you target, showing the included products joined by
"+" separators with a total price and CTA.

**Create it:** **Create Offer → Bundle → Fixed Bundle**. The editor has
three tabs: **Offers block**, **Design**, and **Settings & Schedule**.

## Offers block tab

### When to display

Which product pages *show* the bundle widget (this is separate from which
products are *in* the bundle):

<WhenToDisplay />

### Offer title

The widget heading — default `Bundle & Get 10% OFF!` — with an eye toggle
and the **Add horizontal line on both sides of offer title** checkbox.

### Included products

The actual bundle contents. Search or **Browse** to add products; each row
has a **quantity** field (minimum 1) and a remove button. At least one
product is required.

### Discount

**Discount type:**

- **None**
- **Percentage discount**
- **Fixed amount for each product**
- **Set fixed price for bundle** — the whole bundle sells at one price

plus the **Value** field (currency prefix or `%` suffix; percentage must
be between 0 and 100).

## Design tab

The 21-theme **Themes** carousel, **Layout** (Vertical / Horizontal),
sliders (block padding, block/product/image/CTA border radii, title
strikethrough thickness), 19 color pickers covering every element —
including the "+" separator, footer prices, and CTA — and full
typography controls.

## Settings & Schedule tab

**Combinations**

**Deal settings**

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Pricing | **Show total price**, **Show price per item**, or **Show both per item and total price** (reveals a **Price per unit text** field, default `each`) |
| Let customers choose different variants for each item | Shows a variant selector per bundle item on the storefront |
| Add main item to the cart together with the bundle | Includes the product page's own product alongside the bundle |
| Action | **Behave same as the theme's add to cart button**, **Redirect to cart**, or **Skip cart directly to checkout** |
| Preview out of stock message / Out of stock message | Sold-out display text (default `SOLD OUT`) |
| Footer text | The total row label (default `Total:`) |
| Call to action text | The button label (default `Get this bundle`) |

## Use cases & examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Starter kit on the hero product's page" icon="box-open" description="Camera page shows camera + bag + SD card at 10% off.">
    Target **Specific selected products** (the camera), include all three
    products, use **Percentage discount** `10`. Enable variant choice if
    the bag comes in colors.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Flat-price gift set" icon="gift" description="Three products for exactly $49 with a fixed bundle price.">
    Use **Set fixed price for bundle** with `49` — the widget shows one
    clean price regardless of the items' individual prices.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Frequently-bought-together add-on" icon="cart-plus" description="Keep the main product and add the bundle on top.">
    Enable **Add main item to the cart together with the bundle** so the
    product being viewed joins the bundle in one click.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The bundle doesn't show on the expected page">
    **When to display...** controls *where* the widget appears — it's
    easy to configure the bundle contents but leave targeting on a
    different set of products. Also check the **Yuko Extension** embed
    (red **Not Visible** badge = disabled) and the offer's status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The discount doesn't apply">
    Check **Combinations** against other discounts in the cart, and for
    **Fixed amount for each product** make sure the amount doesn't exceed
    any included product's price.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A bundle item is sold out">
    Enable **Preview out of stock message** to control what customers see
    — otherwise unavailable variants simply can't be added.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
