Quantity Discounts apply tiered discounts **per product**: "Buy 3
quantities of Product A, get 10%. Buy 6, get 20%." Each product's own
quantity is what counts — customers can't combine different products to
reach a tier (for that, use
[Volume discount bundles](/bundles-discounts/offers/volume-discount-bundles)).

**Create it:** **Create Offer → Volume Discounts → Quantity discounts**.
The editor has three tabs: **Offers block**, **Design**, and
**Settings & Schedule**.

## Use cases & examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Case pricing for B2B" icon="boxes-stacked" description="1–5 at list price, 6–11 at 10%, 12+ at 20%.">
    Three tiers with ranges 1–5, 6–11, and 12–no max. Use **Percentage**
    discounts, and leave the last tier's maximum blank for "and above".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Per-unit price drops" icon="arrow-trend-down" description="$8 each up to 5 units, $6.50 each from 6 up.">
    Use **Price per quantity** — each range sets the unit price
    customers pay, shown under the *Price per item* heading.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Storewide 3-for-10% rule" icon="store" description="Every product gets the same per-product tier table.">
    Set **Applies to: All products** — each product page shows the tiers,
    counting only that product's quantity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Customers mixing products don't get the discount">
    That's by design — Quantity Discounts count each product separately.
    If "any 3 items from the collection" is the goal, use
    [Volume discount bundles](/bundles-discounts/offers/volume-discount-bundles)
    instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The widget doesn't render">
    Check the **Yuko Extension** embed (red **Not Visible** badge in the
    Offers list = disabled), the **When to display...** targeting, offer
    status, and the overlap banner for conflicts with other active
    offers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A tier never seems to apply">
    Tier ranges must not overlap and must be unique — the form flags
    "Quantity ranges must not overlap." Check for a gap between ranges
    too: with tiers 1–3 and 6–10, quantities 4–5 get no tier (or the
    nearest lower tier's discount if that setting is on).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
