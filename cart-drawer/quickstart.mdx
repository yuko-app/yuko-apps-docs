This guide sets up the three things most stores want on day one: the
drawer itself, a free-shipping progress bar, and one upsell.

<Info>
  Finish [Installation & theme setup](/cart-drawer/installation) first —
  the theme extension must be Active.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the cart editor">
    In the app, go to **Cart**. The left side lists everything you can
    change (Header, Body, Footer, General). The right side shows a live
    preview — every change appears there instantly, before your real
    store changes.

    <Tip>
      Nothing goes live until you click **Save**. Feel free to explore —
      you can always click **Discard**.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your colors">
    Open **General**. Choose a **Color theme** (Forest green, Black, Red,
    Orange, Tangerine, Lime, Green, Blue, Purple, Magenta, Sage — or
    **Custom** for your exact brand color). Set the **Roundness** to
    match your theme's corner style.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the rewards bar">
    Open **Body → Promotions progress bar**. A default tier is already
    there — set its **Min. price** to your free-shipping threshold (for
    example `50`) and its **Reward type** to **Shipping discount**.

    The bar now tells shoppers things like "Spend $12 more for free
    shipping" and fills up as they add items. See
    [Rewards bar](/cart-drawer/cart/rewards-bar) for tiers, free gifts,
    and confetti.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an upsell">
    Open **Body → Product upsell**. Choose your product source:

    - **Smart AI Recommendations** — Shopify picks related products
      automatically. Zero maintenance.
    - **Choose Upsell Products Manually** — you pick the products, and
      can add a discount to each.

    For a first setup, keep **Smart AI Recommendations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate and save">
    Turn the **Status** toggle at the top to **Active** and click
    **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test on your store">
    Add a product on your storefront.

    <Check>
      The drawer opens with your colors, the progress bar counts toward
      free shipping, and an upsell carousel shows related products.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>
