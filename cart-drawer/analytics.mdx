The **Analytics** page ("Upsell analytics") shows how much extra revenue
the drawer is generating.

## Summary tiles

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Views | How many times upsell products were shown to shoppers |
| Added to Cart | How many times shoppers added an upsell from the drawer |
| Purchases | Upsell items that ended up in paid orders |
| Upsell revenue | Revenue from purchased upsells — in your shop currency and the shopper's (presentment) currency |
| Shipping protection revenue / units sold | What the protection add-on earned |
| Gift wrap revenue | What gift wrap earned |

Purchases and revenue are recorded from Shopify's paid-order data, so
they reflect real completed orders.

## The events table

Every event is logged with its date, type (Upsell Viewed / Added to
Cart / Upsell Purchased / Shipping Protection Purchased / Gift Wrap
Purchased), product, quantity, unit price, revenue, and order. Filter by
**Event type**, **date range**, and **page size**.

<Tip>
  The ratio to watch is **Added to Cart ÷ Views**. Below ~5%, try
  different products, a small discount, or switching the AI
  recommendation type from Related to Complementary.
</Tip>
