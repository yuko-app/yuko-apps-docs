The **Sticky cart** is a floating cart button that stays visible while
shoppers browse — one tap opens the drawer from anywhere. Configure it
on its own page in the app, with its own live preview and enable toggle
("Sticky cart is active / disabled").

## Settings

| Panel | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Icon | Choose from the icon library |
| Shape and size | **Circle**, **Square**, or **Rounded square**, plus **Roundness**, **Shape size**, and **Icon size** |
| Colors | **Shape**, **Icon color**, **Count shape**, **Count color** |
| Extra settings | **Show item count** (the little number bubble), **Hide when cart is empty**, **Display on mobile**, **Position on desktop**, **Position on mobile** (bottom left / bottom right) |

<Tip>
  Keep **Hide when cart is empty** on. The button appears the moment
  something is added — a quiet, effective reminder that a purchase is
  in progress.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  On mobile, check that the button doesn't cover your theme's own
  floating elements (chat widgets usually sit bottom-right — put the
  sticky cart bottom-left if they collide).
</Tip>

Click **Save** to publish, or **Discard changes** to reset.
