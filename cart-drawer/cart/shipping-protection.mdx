**Shipping protection** adds a small paid toggle to the cart — "Protect
my order — $1.95". Shoppers who enable it pay the fee with their order.
Find it under **Footer → Shipping protection**.

## How the charge works

The app creates a hidden Shopify product for the fee and **keeps its
price in sync with your settings** automatically. The panel confirms
with "Auto-managed dummy product (created)" after you enable the section
and click **Save**.

<Warning>
  Don't edit or delete that hidden product in Shopify — the app manages
  it. Deleting it breaks the add-on until the next save recreates it.
</Warning>

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Image, Title, Description | What shoppers see next to the toggle |
| Price / Compare at price | What it costs — the compare-at shows its value |
| Display mode | **Standard** (a row in the footer) or **In checkout button** (built into the button area) |
| Text when it is not in the cart / when it is in the cart | The toggle's two labels |
| Toggle on by default | Protection starts enabled; shoppers can switch it off |
| Automatically remove when the cart is empty | Cleans it up when everything else is removed |

<Tip>
  Price it around 2–3% of your average order value and enable **Toggle
  on by default**. Opt-out keeps it fair — and most shoppers keep it.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Shipping protection is a support-unlocked section. If you see a
  padlock, click **Request feature** and we'll enable it for your store.
</Note>

## Tracking revenue

Protection revenue and units sold have their own tiles on the
[Analytics](/cart-drawer/analytics) page, so you can see exactly what
this module earns.
