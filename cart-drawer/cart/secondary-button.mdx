The **Secondary button** is an optional second button near the checkout
button, usually "Continue shopping". Find it under **Footer → Secondary
button**.

## Settings

- Button text and **Text color** / styling
- **Button action**:
  - **Close cart drawer** — back to browsing where they were
  - **Redirect to checkout page**
  - **Redirect to custom URL** — e.g. your bestsellers collection

<Tip>
  "Continue shopping" with **Close cart drawer** is the safe default.
  Pointing it at a collection URL turns the button into a merchandising
  tool — good during sales.
</Tip>
