The **Footer strip** is one small text line below everything else in the
drawer. Find it under **Footer → Footer strip**.

## Settings

- **Text** — the message
- **Styles** — font size (px) and background

<Tip>
  The classic use: "Taxes and shipping calculated at checkout." It
  answers the "why doesn't this match my card statement?" question
  before it's asked.
</Tip>
