**Products in cart** controls how the items themselves appear. Find it
under **Body → Products in cart**.

## Display options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Sort products by | **Cart order (default)**, **Price: low to high**, or **Price: high to low** |
| Show variant selector | Lets shoppers switch size or color right in the cart |
| Show quantity selector | Plus/minus controls on each line |
| Show single item price instead of total price | Shows each unit's price instead of the line total |
| Image size / Vertical spacing | Sizing for the product rows |

**Styles** cover text, badge, and card background colors.

<Tip>
  Keep the variant and quantity selectors on. Every fix a shopper can
  make inside the drawer (wrong size, wrong amount) is a trip back to
  the product page you just saved them.
</Tip>

## Subscriptions

If you sell subscriptions, cart rows can show plan details and invite
upgrades:

- **Info text** with shortcodes for the **plan name**, **plan price**,
  and **frequency** — e.g. "Ships every 30 days"
- **Show subscription upgrade** — a prompt inviting one-time buyers to
  switch to a subscription, with your **Subscription upgrade text**
- **Allow changing subscription** — lets shoppers switch between
  one-time purchase and subscription plans in the cart
- Style controls for the plan and upgrade elements (backgrounds, text,
  borders)

<Tip>
  The in-cart upgrade prompt is one of the cheapest subscription
  conversion points you have — the shopper already chose the product;
  you're only changing *how* they buy it.
</Tip>
