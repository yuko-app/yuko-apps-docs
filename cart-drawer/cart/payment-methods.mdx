**Payment methods** shows a row of payment brand icons in the footer —
quiet proof that checkout is safe and their payment method is accepted.
Find it under **Footer → Payment methods**.

## Settings

- Toggle each brand icon on or off — Amex, Apple Pay, Amazon Pay,
  Affirm, Alipay, and many more
- **Add custom icon** — upload any icon we don't ship (local payment
  methods, BNPL providers), managed under **Custom icons**

<Tip>
  Show only what you actually accept — 4 to 6 icons reads as
  trustworthy, a wall of 15 reads as clutter.
</Tip>
