The footer's add-on modules sit just above the checkout button. Each has
its own toggle, and footer modules can be dragged to reorder.

## Shipping protection

A small paid add-on ("Protect my order — $1.95") shoppers can toggle on.
It's real revenue: the app creates a hidden Shopify product for the
charge and **keeps its price in sync with your settings** automatically.

Configure: the image, **Title**, **Description**, **Price**, and
optional **Compare at price**. Then the extra settings:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Display mode | **Standard** (a row in the footer) or **In checkout button** (built into the button area) |
| Text when it is not in the cart / when it is in the cart | The two states of the toggle label |
| Toggle on by default | Protection starts enabled — shoppers can switch it off |
| Automatically remove when the cart is empty | Cleans it up if everything else is removed |

<Note>
  The hidden product is created when you enable Shipping Protection and
  click **Save** — the panel confirms with "Auto-managed dummy product
  (created)". Don't edit or delete that product in Shopify; the app
  manages it.
</Note>

<Note>
  Shipping protection is a support-unlocked section — click **Request
  feature** if you see a padlock.
</Note>

## Gift wrap

Works the same way: an optional paid add-on with an image, title, and
price, charged through an auto-managed hidden product. Great around
holidays.

## Order notes

A simple text box for special instructions — set the **Title** and the
**Placeholder** text. Notes arrive on the Shopify order as the standard
order note.

## Discount codes

Let shoppers apply discount codes without leaving the cart:

- **Discount title**, **Button label**, and **Error message** (shown
  when a code fails)
- **Show remove control on discount tag** — a small ✕ on the applied
  code chip
- **Available coupons** — search your store's Shopify discount codes
  and pick which ones to *show* in the cart. Shoppers see the selected
  codes (with their minimums, like "Min. order $30") and can apply them
  with one tap.

<Tip>
  The **Available coupons** list turns a passive input field into a
  promotion. Showing "WELCOME10 — no minimum" converts far better than
  hoping shoppers remember a code.
</Tip>

## Use cases & examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Protection as default" icon="shield-halved" description="Shipping protection on by default at a small price point.">
    Price it around 2–3% of your average order, enable **Toggle on by
    default**, and use the compare-at price to show its value. Opt-out
    keeps it fair; most shoppers keep it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Holiday gift-wrap season" icon="gift" description="Gift wrap on from November, off in January.">
    Upload a festive image, set your price, and enable it seasonally —
    the hidden product's price stays in sync automatically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="One visible welcome coupon" icon="ticket" description="Show WELCOME10 in every cart for first-time visitors.">
    Add your welcome code in **Available coupons**. One tap to apply
    keeps the momentum toward checkout.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The protection/gift-wrap product shows up in my store admin">
    That's expected — it's the hidden product used to charge the fee.
    Leave it alone: the app keeps it in sync and hides it from your
    storefront. Deleting it breaks the add-on until the next save
    recreates it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A discount code won't apply">
    The error message you configured is shown when Shopify rejects the
    code — usually an expired code or an unmet minimum. Check the code's
    rules in Shopify's Discounts section.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
