This page covers the drawer's header and the smaller body sections. Each
one has its own toggle, its own styles, and a translation bar for
multi-language stores.

## Top bar (Header)

The drawer's header. Choose between two tabs:

- **Text** — a short line like "Your cart". Shortcodes can insert the
  live **number of items in cart** and **cart total price**.
- **Logo** — upload your logo instead, with **Logo size** and **Logo
  roundness** controls.

Styles: alignment (Left / Center / Right), font size, and background.

<Tip>
  "Your cart (`{{item_count}}`)" is a small touch that makes the drawer
  feel alive — the number updates as shoppers add items.
</Tip>

## Announcement bar

A slim colored strip for a short message — "Free returns for 30 days" or
"Order by 2pm for same-day dispatch". Just the **Text** plus background
and text colors.

<Note>
  This is a support-unlocked section — click **Request feature** if you
  see a padlock, and we'll enable it for your store.
</Note>

## Savings banner

Shows shoppers the total they're saving — "You're saving $14!". Use the
`{{savings}}` shortcode to insert the amount (calculated from compare-at
prices minus current prices).

<Tip>
  This banner only earns its space if your products have **compare-at
  prices** set in Shopify. No compare-at prices, no savings to show.
</Tip>

## Products in cart

The line items themselves. Options:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Sort products by | **Cart order (default)**, **Price: low to high**, or **Price: high to low** |
| Show variant selector | Lets shoppers switch size/color right in the cart |
| Show quantity selector | Plus/minus controls per line |
| Show single item price instead of total price | Shows each unit's price instead of the line total |
| Image size / Vertical spacing | Sizing controls for the product rows |

**Subscriptions:** if you sell subscriptions, the rows can show the plan
name, price, and frequency (via shortcodes), plus an optional
**subscription upgrade** prompt and an **Allow changing subscription**
toggle so shoppers can switch between one-time and subscription.

## Timer

A countdown that creates urgency — "Your cart is reserved for 10:00
minutes". Set the duration (in minutes), the text (with a countdown
shortcode), and colors. There's also a stronger option: **Remove all
cart items when timer ends**.

<Warning>
  Emptying the cart on expiry is aggressive — shoppers who stepped away
  lose their picks. Most stores do better with the timer as a visual
  nudge only.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Timer is a support-unlocked section — click **Request feature** to
  have it enabled.
</Note>

## Trust badges

Small reassurance icons with titles — "Secure checkout", "Free
returns", "24/7 support". Click **Add badge** for each one, pick an
icon, set the **Title**, and adjust icon size and colors. You can also
**Show it under checkout button** for maximum effect where the decision
happens.

<Tip>
  Three badges is the sweet spot. One is lonely, five is noise.
</Tip>
