**Trust badges** are small icons with short titles that answer the quiet
doubts shoppers have before paying — "Secure checkout", "Free returns",
"24/7 support". Find them under **Body → Trust badges**.

## Settings

- **Add badge** — each badge has an icon (from the picker) and a
  **Title**
- **Icon size** and colors under **Styles**
- **Show it under checkout button** — moves the badges right where the
  payment decision happens

<Tip>
  Three badges is the sweet spot. One is lonely, five is noise. Pick the
  three concerns your customers actually email you about.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Show it under checkout button** is usually the stronger placement —
  reassurance lands best at the exact moment of hesitation.
</Tip>
