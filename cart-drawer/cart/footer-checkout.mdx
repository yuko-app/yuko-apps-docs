These modules make up the bottom of the drawer — the part that turns a
full cart into an order. All of them are drag-to-reorder.

## Subtotal row

Shows the cart's subtotal. Configure the **Subtotal Text** and the
**Savings Text** — the label for the savings line that appears above the
subtotal when a discount is applied.

## Discounts row

Displays applied discounts as their own line, so shoppers see the deal
reflected before checkout.

## Checkout button

The star of the drawer:

- Button text with the `{{cart_total}}` shortcode — e.g.
  `Checkout • {{cart_total}}`
- **Show terms and conditions** with your **Terms text**
- Full styling: colors, roundness, font

<Tip>
  Putting the live total on the button ("Checkout • $64.90") removes
  the last surprise between cart and payment — it consistently helps
  conversion.
</Tip>

**GoKwik stores:** if the GoKwik app is installed on your storefront,
enable the **GoKwik checkout flow** to hand the button off to GoKwik
one-click checkout. If GoKwik isn't detected, the button safely falls
back to the native checkout.

## Accelerated checkout buttons

Express buttons (Shop Pay, PayPal, and similar) shown near the checkout
button. You can size them separately for desktop and mobile, and **Make
buttons collapsible** (optionally collapsed by default) to keep the
footer tidy.

## Payment methods

A row of payment icons for reassurance. Toggle each brand on or off
(Amex, Apple Pay, Amazon Pay, Affirm, Alipay, and many more), and use
**Add custom icon** to upload any icon we don't ship.

<Tip>
  Show only what you actually accept — 4 to 6 icons reads as
  trustworthy, a wall of 15 reads as clutter.
</Tip>

## Footer strip

A final small text line below everything — a good home for "Taxes and
shipping calculated at checkout".

## Secondary button

An optional second button next to checkout, usually "Continue
shopping". Pick its **Button action**:

- **Close cart drawer** — back to browsing
- **Redirect to checkout page**
- **Redirect to custom URL** — e.g. your collections page

## Empty cart state

Under the preview's **Empty cart** mode you design what shoppers see
when the cart has nothing in it: the icon, the **Empty message**, and an
optional **continue shopping button** with its own text, colors, and
action (close the drawer or go to a custom URL).

<Tip>
  Don't leave the empty cart as a dead end. "Your cart is empty — see
  what's new" with a button to your bestsellers turns a bounce into a
  browse.
</Tip>
