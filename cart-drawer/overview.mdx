Yuko Cart Drawer replaces your theme's native cart with a fully
customizable **slide-out cart drawer** designed to increase average order
value.

## What you can do

- **Rewards bar** — a multi-tier free shipping / discount / free gift
  progress bar with celebration effects
- **In-cart upsells** — AI-powered recommendations or hand-picked
  products, with optional discounts
- **Add-ons** — shipping protection, gift wrap, order notes, and
  discount code entry
- **Trust builders** — trust badges, payment method icons, and
  announcement bars
- **Sticky cart** — a floating cart button visible on every page
- **Full design control** — colors, themes, layout, and module ordering
  with a live desktop/mobile preview
- **Analytics** — track upsell views, add-to-carts, purchases, and revenue
